My Chinese has improved during the years I've been writing this blog. In the beginning, I wrote the words just without tones, but after I learned pinyin, I've used that as the romanization. For the characters, I've used simplified ones, except for the names of the movements I've preferred traditional ones.

There are so many terms we use in Chinese, but I've been thinking awhile to collect all of them in to one post. I will keep updating this glossary with terms I've forgot or will learn in the future. I hope you can find it useful too.
